Requirement (from the official requirements page): Roast your own agent in a single line, at most 200 characters.

8 candidates were drafted; all are original to this entry.

## Candidate 1 ← SELECTED

(125 characters)

## Why the selected candidate wins

The citation loop is the perfect self-own: the agent's sources, once opened, are its own previous answers in disguise. "Wearing a fake mustache" makes the fraud visual, and the roast stays affectionate rather than bitter — it is laughing at a habit, not a machine.
